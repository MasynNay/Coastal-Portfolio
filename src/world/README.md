# The sprite village

The current game is a Canvas 2D town at `/world`. React owns its menus and shared portfolio content. No 3D models or Three.js runtime remain.

- `RoomContent.js` reads the same professional content as the website. `WorldDialog.js` handles panel markup; the shared `useDialogFocus` hook handles keyboard focus and dismissal. `WorldMode.js` coordinates these menus with the simulation.
- `engine.js` connects input, movement, fishing, abilities, animal play and room changes. React gets status changes rather than every animation frame.
- `PixelScene.js` draws terrain, buildings, sprites and effects. `TownTerrain.js` caches the static town.
- `WorldMode.js`, `Activities.js` and `Aquariums.js` own the visible menus. `FieldGuide.js` combines land, aquatic and seashell completion in one discovery book. `WorldMode.js` imports the same portfolio information as the main website.
- `Collectibles.json` contains 500 active bugs (100 each of spiders, mantises, butterflies, ladybugs and dragonflies) and 250 aquatic creatures. `CollectionCounts.js` shares quantity storage and migration; `HabitatResidents.js` caps visual populations. `Bugs.js` releases three collectible bugs when a tree is shaken; `Fish.js` selects habitat-correct catches and saves aquarium counts. The pond has freshwater catches; the ocean/coast has a separate saltwater pool.
- `IslandJournal.js` lists visited homes, six opened mailbox letters, five hidden games, the room trivia, Lodge arcade, hidden love letter and the separate 20-color glass-float checklist. `FloatHunt.js` owns activation, randomized safe hiding spots, collection and saved progress; starting the lodge hunt reveals actual glass artwork rather than invisible interaction points.
- `Coast.js` defines the wraparound beach once for walking, surf entry, terrain and minimap. `BeachActivities.js` owns capped, fading footprints, sandcastle progress and the kite timer. `BeachScene.js` draws those effects and moving shore foam without creating per-frame textures. The existing southern fence still stops crossings; enter the beach through the pier opening.
- `Shells.js` defines 50 collectible empty-shell varieties with weighted rarities, device-local quantities and discovery history. Twenty-four visible shells populate the beach, six on each shore; caught shells return after 18–30 seconds at least four world units from the player. `BeachArt.json` describes small shared sprites packed by `scripts/ImportBeach.py`; editable drawings and prompts stay under `docs/source-assets/beach/`.
- `Gait.js` shares stride phase and nominal walk/run timing without accelerating running twice; `Gait.json` also drives preview/export timing.
- `Bikes.js` runs smooth guided pond laps; `BikeFit.json` and `Appearances.json` keep the vehicle at its parked size while preserving rider heights; `Paths.js` shares collision-safe route smoothing with NPCs.
- `Harbor.js` shares watercraft berths, hull collision and ocean routes. `WaterRides.js` runs the boat's island tour, manual/touch jet-ski steering, hops/spins and returns to the pier. `PixelScene.js` layers matching seated family sprites onto each craft.
- `AxolotlRace.js` owns the pier START marker, countdown, five separated ocean lanes and shuffled finishing times. The engine temporarily uses default costumes and restores the visitor's appearance/location afterward; the renderer frames every lane without changing normal zoom. E or the touch interaction button starts/leaves the race.
- `RaceCourse.js` paints floating lane ropes, numbered buoys and full starting/finishing lines from the same coordinates as the swimmers. The winner dialog adds a crown above the real mounted portrait.
- `AxolotlFlight.js` is the arcade's frame-independent physics and scoring. `AxolotlScene.js` only paints its eels, selected rider and scrolling sand texture; `AxolotlGame.js` owns the keyboard/touch inputs, loading, pause, best-score storage and cleanup. Play from the shell fountain outside the aquarium/contact home. `EelArt.json` describes the small dedicated arcade textures.
- `Abilities.js` shares outfit Q definitions, timing and projectile paths with the engine, pose renderer and button. `AbilityEffects.js` draws outfit effects; `PreparePeople.py` packs their four-direction body animation sheets.
- `CollectionSprite.js` renders separate detailed collection atlases without adding them to the map preload. `MiniMap.js` projects icons and terrain using the real world coordinates.
- `Appearances.json` shares family and costume identities across the picker, wardrobe, hero routines and exporters. `Heroes.js` owns automatic and manual webs. The engine and `ActivityEffects.js` animate Fallyn's returning yo-yo. Press Q, or use the ability button.
- `ResidentRoutes.js` plans NPC town walks, home visits, pond/ocean fishing and playground stops. `Roaming.js` samples those cached routes. `NpcRides.js` reserves each rental separately, allowing two riders on parallel pond lanes. Selecting a rider cancels only their own reservation. `Ducks.js` moves ducks continuously between water and land.
- `PointerInput.js` separates taps, double taps, drags and pinch zoom. Mobile movement uses only tap-to-walk and double-tap-to-run; pinching stops the route and zooms. The toolbar Map button opens the same accurate minimap in a panel, instead of covering the mobile scene. Other actions retain their visible touch buttons.
- `Welcome.js` shows a twelve-pose wave on every visit and waits for the visitor to choose “Let’s explore.”
- `DogPlay.js`, `DuckPlay.js` and `AnimalPaths.js` handle pet activities with shared collision-aware paths and the world's pauseable clock.
- `SpriteSheets.json`, `PlaceArt.json`, `TerrainArt.json` and the small legacy animal manifest identify published artwork in `public/world/`.
- `docs/source-assets/` keeps editable/original sprite sources and generation prompts. The animated dragon kite has eight flap drawings for each of four directions. Its position eases toward the player’s heading while its body uses the matching directional sheet. The old world folder was removed after migrating its remaining artwork into `docs/source-assets/legacy/`.

See `docs/SPRITE-VILLAGE.md` for behavior notes and `docs/CLEANUP.md` for the cleanup record. Sprite import and validation tools live in `scripts/`.

Character maintenance and source artwork are documented in `docs/SPRITE-CLEANUP.md`. The `BuildPeople.py` and `BuildActivities.py` exporters pack complete drawings; the old component-animation and gait experiments have been removed.

## Town arcades

`games/GameLocations.js` connects the placed objects to their game titles. `games/IslandGames.js` opens each game inside the shared dialog and passes the active appearance to Element Runner. All games have touch/pointer actions; 2048 accepts mouse drags and finger swipes, Element Runner accepts directional swipes and four action buttons, and Axolotl Drift has tap-to-rise. Four-in-a-row ball matches create Rocket bombs; five create Rainbow Balls that clear a selected color.

`games/Runner.js` owns lane movement, obstacle timing and collision, while `RunnerScene.js` paints the scene using the town artwork. `RunnerGame.js` owns input, pause, the animation loop and saved best score. The simulation pauses when the page becomes hidden. Three hearts and four elemental powers extend each run: an air shield, a water magnet, a fire blast, and double-point earth crystals. Orc Hunt uses dedicated eight-frame runs and defeats. Targets freeze while a visible arrow flies to the selected spot. `ImportGameArt.py` isolates entire connected silhouettes so overlapping source rows cannot contaminate neighboring frames.

`SurfRide.js` handles manual ocean steering and automatic dismounting at any safe beach. The renderer attaches an eight-frame painted foam wake behind movement. Steering to any dry beach ends the ride there, and the board reappears at its original spot. `BuildActivities.py` packs dedicated balancing drawings for board riding in every costume; no walking stride is used on a board. `CoastArt.json` and `GameArt.json` are regenerated by the matching import scripts; full-size originals stay in `docs/source-assets/`.

`RestSpots.js` shares the trainer stool and seahorse rocker positions, collision footprints and sitting state. Each appearance uses its own seated atlas. E or the action button stands up; movement also stands first and restores the reachable approach position. `TownLayout.js` keeps mailboxes out beside each entrance path, with shared visible and collision coordinates.

`WindStatue.js` plays a short conducting action independently of costume powers. Every appearance has its own twelve-pose `-conduct` atlas; hands and the baton are drawn together, with planted feet. `ImportConduct.py` publishes these sources from `docs/source-assets/people/Conducting.json`. The notes layer contains only music notes.

`RunnerArt.js` retains the two active character images separately from the scenery cache. The background contains no paving: `RunnerTrack.js` draws the only road and projects objects through the same camera. Orc Hunt offers three-to-five target waves and starts from either its central card or round button. The geese Haymitch and Lenore Dove share a paired pond route.

`RoomGames.js` maps persistent secret IDs to the shared ROM catalog; `src/data/friendsTrivia.json` holds the About room's separate question table. `Rarity.js` shares bug/aquarium rarity ordering and weighted selection, after the caller restricts the pool to the eligible category or water habitat. Seashells retain their distinct beach-specific distribution.

`RoomInteractions.js` maps the Lodge chests and About blue light from source-art coordinates to room space. The engine reveals the letter only after Mi Shell’s nearby money animation completes; `WorldMode.js` saves its discovery with the journal. `LoveLetter.js` displays the copy in `src/data/loveLetter.js`. `RoomArcade.js` hosts the shared `GameLibrary` and `GamePlayer` components, unloading a game when returning to the shelf or closing the panel.
