Current parked designs use `Bikes.png` and `ImportBikes.py`. Current rider artwork is in `../actions/Riding-*.png`, published by `PreparePeople.py`. The older rider sheets below remain the pose/design references supplied to that refinement.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. `Bikes.png` defines the two bike designs. Each `Bike-<character>.png` references that sheet and the corresponding family member's sprite. `ImportBikes.py` packs separate four-direction Acro and Mach atlases for parking and each rider. The rider and bike are drawn together to keep hands, pedals and wheels aligned.

Bikes

Use case: stylized-concept. Production overhead GBA-style bicycle sprite atlas inspired by the Acro and Mach bikes in Pokemon Emerald. Exactly FOUR COLUMNS by EIGHT ROWS, 32 full bicycle sprites on a transparent background. NO RIDERS OR PEOPLE. Clean readable detailed pixel-art consistent with an overhead coastal JRPG town.
Rows1-4 BLUE ACRO BIKE: compact blue BMX frame, high chrome handlebars, thick black tires, small comfortable saddle. Rows5-8 RED MACH BIKE: slender scarlet racing frame, lower chrome handlebars, narrow black tires, small dark saddle. Distinct silhouettes as well as colors.
Directions within each group of four rows: DOWN/front, UP/back, LEFT/side, RIGHT/side. Every cell in a row must maintain that exact facing direction. Four frames per row form a subtle looping animation: wheel spokes and pedal crank rotate through four positions; bicycle frame and wheel centers stay perfectly fixed. Both complete wheels always visible in side view. Correct front/back foreshortening, not eight copies of the same side-view bike.
Equal-size cells, generous transparent gutters, no cropping of tires or handlebars, no extra wheels, stands, text, logos, labels, ground shadows, grid lines, scenery or background. True alpha transparency.
