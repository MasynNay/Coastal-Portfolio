More than a place to write lyrics. A place to find the song.

NO RULES FOR HOW TO BEGIN / 001
THE CREATIVE PROCESS / 01—03

Start anywhere.
Follow the sound.

You don't need to know music theory or arrive with a finished idea. Chordlore helps you shape the process without taking over the creative decisions.

01 /

Put the idea into words.

Start with a story, lyrics you already wrote, a personal memory, or a made-up prompt. Work in whatever order feels natural.

02 /

Find its musical shape.

Explore melody, rhythm, and chord sketches. Rearrange the parts until the idea starts to sound like the one in your head.

03 /

Record. Refine. Keep it.

Make vocal takes where your browser permits, experiment with a mix, and export a backup so your work is never tied to this website.

MADE FOR THE PERSON BEHIND THE MUSIC

The best songs don't sound like everyone else.
They sound like you.

Chordlore is built around one idea: the person making the music matters more than the software. Use all the guidance you want, or ignore it and create your own way.

INSIDE THE WORKSPACE

The tools are there.
The direction is yours.

A connected creative workspace for the moments between the first idea and a song you can take with you.

01 WRITE

Story & lyrics

Organize your thoughts, shape lyrical sections, and keep the original words that matter most.

02 COMPOSE

Melody & arrangement

Build musical sketches using notes, harmony, beat patterns, and a timeline you can change.

03 FINISH

Record & export

Capture takes where supported, experiment with a mix, and create local files or project backups.

ABOUT THE BROWSER PREVIEW

This is a working, early portfolio preview, not a finished audio production service. No account is required. Your projects and recordings are stored in your browser on this device, not in an online account. Clearing site data or using private browsing can remove them. Export project backups you want to keep.

Microphone access is optional and controlled by your browser. The public web edition does not include the desktop native audio engine, local AI bridge, cloud sync, or all desktop capabilities.
