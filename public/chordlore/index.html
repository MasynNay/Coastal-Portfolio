Good to know

Made for experimenting.

This is an early portfolio preview, not a finished recording service. It works best in a modern desktop browser over HTTPS. Some features available in the desktop app, including its native audio engine and local AI bridge, are not available on the public website.

Your work belongs to you

Save what matters.

Projects and recordings are stored in your browser on this device, not in an online account. Clearing browser storage or using private browsing may erase them. Use the studio's project export to create a backup you control.

Microphone access is optional and requested by your browser when you choose to record. This preview does not provide cloud sync or automatic AI song generation.
