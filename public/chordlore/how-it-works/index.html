Make music, even if
you've never made music.

You don't need a microphone, an instrument or music theory to get started. The free browser edition of Chordlore can turn one line you wrote into an editable instrumental song sketch. It's not a full AI-produced recording, but it gives your first idea a sound you can keep.

Start with one line ↗

No registration or audio file uploads needed to try Song Starter.

The three steps

01 / WORDS

Write one line

Write something that's yours: a memory, a feeling, or an imagined scene. It can be as simple as “I left the porch light on.” No rhymes or verse structure needed.

02 / SOUND

Pick a direction

Choose a warm, energetic, spacious or cinematic arrangement. Chordlore creates an original synthesized melody, chords, drums and musical sections you can change.

03 / KEEP IT

Listen and download

Press Hear your sketch, then download the instrumental as a WAV file. Save a project backup as well so you can return to the idea later.

Common questions

What you can actually make

Does Chordlore generate a finished song with a singer?

Not in the current public Song Starter. It creates an original instrumental music sketch using synthesized sounds. Your exact line is placed in editable lyric sections as a placeholder; it isn't automatically sung. You can record your own voice in supported browsers or work on the composition further.

Do I need to make or upload any audio files?

No. Song Starter makes its own accompaniment in your browser. You can listen to the result immediately, then click Download instrumental WAV to get an ordinary audio file. Imported music and recordings are optional extras.

Do I need an account or pay anything?

No account or payment is required to try the browser preview. Projects are saved locally in the browser on the device where you created them. There is no cloud account or automatic cross-device sync yet.

What happens if I close my browser?

Projects normally remain in the same browser's local storage, but private-browsing modes, deleting site data or storage problems can erase them. Use Back up song project to save a copy you can import later.

How much musical experience do I need?

None to create and hear an initial sketch. Advanced arrangement, recording and mixing tools are available if you decide to go further, but the starter itself only requires one written line and a sound choice.
