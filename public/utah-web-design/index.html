Frequently asked questions

Before you hire a web designer

Do you only take projects in northern Utah?

No. I work with small businesses in Weber, Davis, and Utah counties, across Utah, and remotely throughout the United States.

Do we have to meet in person?

No. I offer an email-first process with a written scope and approvals. I do not advertise a public customer-facing office.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes, depending on the platform and access available. Describe what isn't working in the inquiry form, and I'll review the scope before quoting.

Will I own my domain and website?

Domain and hosting accounts stay under your control. The written proposal explains ownership, source deliverables, any licenses, and ongoing service costs before the project begins.

How much does a small-business website cost?

Pricing guidance currently starts at $1,500 for a typical small-business website, with a tailored written quote before any deposit. Complex tools and integrations may cost more.
